01 — About the project

A small piece of light hardware,
driven like an instrument.

LED Matrix is a research build that turns a Waveshare P2 64×64 panel into a playable visual instrument. TouchDesigner generates frames on a laptop; a Raspberry Pi Zero 2W receives them over ArtNet UDP and pushes them straight to the Adafruit RGB Matrix Bonnet at line rate.

The project explores how far a tiny SBC can be pushed when paired with a desktop visual engine — and how to design effects whose grammar is legible at 64×64. Every visual is built twice: once as a GLSL/Python pipeline in TD, and once as a standalone Python runner so the Pi can perform without a host.

04 — Signal flow

From node graph
to physical photon.

Each frame is a 12 288-byte payload — 64 × 64 × 3 bytes of RGB — chopped into 24 sequentially-numbered ArtNet universes and unicast to the Pi. On the receiving end, a single Python process reassembles universes, writes into the rpi-rgb-led-matrix offscreen canvas, and swaps it on vsync.

Latency from a slider move in TouchDesigner to a pixel changing on the wall is one frame at 60 Hz, plus one Ethernet hop — measured at ≈ 22 ms end to end.

06 — Findings from the dev log

Things that silently broke
before they worked.

A condensed selection from the dev log — the kind of behaviour you only learn about by staring at a black panel for an hour at 2 AM.

TouchDesigner

scriptTOP output is frozen — never updates.

With no wired TOP input, TD assumes inputs are unchanged and skips re-cooking. Fix: feed a noiseTOP with tz = absTime.seconds into input 0 to force a cook every frame.

FFT

audiospectrum frequency indexing was completely wrong.

With frequencylog = 1.0, samples are log-distributed — what we labelled "60 Hz" actually queried ~20 Hz. Switch to linear; idx = int(freq_hz) works directly.

Numpy

scriptTOP Y-axis is inverted on write.

copyNumpyArray uses OpenGL convention: arr[0] = bottom. Bars filling arr[H-bar_px:H] grow downward. Fill arr[0:bar_px] instead.

DSP

Bars stuck at 100% — hard clipping kills dynamics.

Replaced min(1.0, raw*gain) with arctan soft-compression. Loud signals approach the ceiling without collapsing onto it.

Hardware

Display showed two half-height repeats.

HUB75E panels need the 5th address line. Bridged the Bonnet's 16/E/8 jumper — middle pad to 8 — and the second half snapped into place.

Pi Zero

LED flicker during animation.

BCM2835 PWM conflict with rpi-rgb-led-matrix's hardware pulse train. Disabled in /boot/firmware/config.txt: dtoverlay=vc4-kms-v3d,noaudio. Steady from then on.
